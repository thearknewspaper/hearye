• The main window now shows your version and edition, with a link to check for updates.

From 2.4:
• Pick your boards: paste a city's meetings page and HearYe lists its boards, including ones with nothing posted lately. Follow only the ones you want, and change them any time with Boards… beside the source. If a new board appears, HearYe alerts you and leaves it off until you tick it.
• Paste a city page that embeds its Granicus archive, or an archive with agendas but no recordings.

From 2.3.2:
• Follows the links inside agenda PDFs, including Adobe Acrobat share links, so Belvedere's staff reports and minutes are saved with the agenda under their item numbers.
• Finds Granicus meetings listed under Upcoming Events but left out of the agenda feed, such as Tiburon's Town Council.
• Agendas uploaded as PDFs are saved as PDFs, and agenda files are no longer labeled with a stray number.

From 2.3.1:
• Updates download and install themselves: HearYe fetches a new version in the background, checks that it's signed and notarized, and swaps it in when you quit (or click Install and Relaunch in About HearYe). Turn it off there.
• Agendas and full packets: each watched meeting gets its own folder, with every document named for its agenda item, saved as soon as the agenda is posted. At meeting time HearYe checks again for late mail and other additions, and the recording's audio joins the same folder.
• Watches PrimeGov's newer "OneMeeting" portals, such as Marin County's.
• Watch the meeting systems cities and school districts actually use: Swagit, Legistar, PrimeGov, CivicClerk, BoardDocs, ProudCity city sites and Cablecast pages, alongside Granicus and YouTube. Paste the system's public page and HearYe finds the recordings.
• Granicus and Swagit player pages now download directly.
• When a page can't be watched, HearYe says why and what to paste instead.
